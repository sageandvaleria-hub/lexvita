Strategic Implications — Lekki–Apapa Corridor

Freight strategy: fix now, don't ride spot

Lock in period or index-linked freight agreements with floors (on the Seanergy/Himalaya model) rather than accept spot exposure in a consolidating, risk-inflated market.

War-risk and security planning is a line item

Any Nigerian cargo transiting Hormuz, Bab al-Mandab or Black Sea lanes needs explicit war-risk cover, route vetting and contingency planning. Treat all third-party transit-service approaches as potential fraud.

Asset management: monetise peak values, buy selectively

Nigerian owners with ageing tonnage should consider selling at cycle highs to fund modern dual-fuel/scrubber-fitted replacements; buyers should only acquire with firm employment attached.
