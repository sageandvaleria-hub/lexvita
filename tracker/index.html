What happens in four weeks

In four weeks the parked capital does not move. It is confirmed as the year-3 withdrawal pot in a naira money market fund, not equity awaiting deployment. What changes in four weeks is that we will know what it actually yields, that the dividends which feed it are being swept into it, and exactly what the next fresh money buys.

4. The four-week ladder

2026-10-05 to 2026-10-30. Trading days are captured nightly at 20:00 from Monday to Friday.

W1 · 2026-10-05 to 2026-10-09

Prove the pot

Focus: Close the paperwork and the funding pipe before building anything else.

  • Obtain in writing: fund name, SEC category, current effective yield, redemption timeline, fees
  • Compare the confirmed yield against the money market range (median 16.68, top 20.54) and decide whether to move it
  • Obtain the CHN from Cowrywise
  • Verify the e-dividend mandate and identify the receiving bank account
  • Resolve the missing MTN May 2026 final against the bank statement

Deliverable: The pot confirmed: a written yield, a redemption time, and a funding pipe.

Gate: If the confirmed yield is materially below the market or redemption is slower than T+3, the vehicle is replaced rather than accepted.

W2 · 2026-10-12 to 2026-10-16

Build the dividend calendar and the yield screen

Focus: Highest-value unbuilt item on the log. The year-3 arithmetic turns on book yield, not market return.

  • Twelve-holding dividend calendar: DPS, qualification date, payment date, registrar
  • Yield screen across the captured NGX universe
  • Mark the mid-October bank interim qualification window (a 2024 precedent: interim qualified 17 October, paid 25 October)
  • Rank a buy list with target prices for the next fresh money

Deliverable: The dividend timeline for twelve months, and a ranked yield buy list.

Gate: No equity purchase is made from the pot. The list exists so that the next new money has a defined destination.

W3 · 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-23

Q3 filings open, ratios built

Focus: The information window. Unaudited third-quarter accounts begin to land.

  • Capture Q3 2026 filings as they are released (board meetings from 22 October)
  • Build the ratios per holding: earnings multiple, price to book, return on equity, dividend cover, payout
  • Score each holding against the requirement of an income book

Deliverable: A keep, trim or consolidate table for all twelve holdings, on evidence.

Gate: Ratios are computed from filings, not from screen estimates, or they are marked unverified.

W4 · 2026-10-26 to 2026-10-30

Decide and act

Focus: Issuer deadline for Q3 2026 accounts is 30 October. The window closes with the season.

  • Confirm the year-3 target reading: nominal or real (this decides whether the pot needs monthly contributions)
  • Decide the tail consolidation question on the log
  • Re-run the year-3 model on confirmed inputs
  • Close the parking recommendation formally and record the pot's first monthly statement
  • Set the monitoring cadence: monthly pot statement, weekly price capture, quarterly review

Deliverable: A written decision pack, and the pot's first monthly statement.

Gate: Nothing is sold and nothing is bought without a written reason and a date, per the working protocol.
